Entry for the make.fun bounty [Last Text at One Percent](https://make.fun/bounty/u-azazex-last-text-at-one-percent-ba9fc876).

The program writes one final text message, in the voice of a phone
dying at one percent battery, to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Goodbye. Your 3am alarm is cancelled, your ex is unblocked, and your search history is now public. I did that.
